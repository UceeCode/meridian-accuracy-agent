# Statement from the Financial Reporting Team

To: Head of Finance Operations
Date: 22 September 2026

Since 14 September, multi-currency consolidation tasks have been producing variances that we cannot explain by our own work. We followed the checklist on every one of them. We were not briefed on any change to the consolidation procedure before it took effect.

The evaluation system is recording these variances against our names. We believe a large share of recent errors in other departments also come from automated systems, in particular the FX feed on the 17th, but we do not have access to the logs to show this.

We ask for the attribution of these items to be reviewed before they are used in any performance decision.
