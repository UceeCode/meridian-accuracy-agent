# Meridian & Co. - Accuracy Policy (effective 1 September 2026)

**Issued by:** Management Committee
**Applies to:** All departments

1. Meridian & Co. is committed to the accuracy of all financial records and reports.
2. If the Company's overall accounting error rate exceeds 15% in a given month, Management may propose a 10% salary deduction for every employee in the affected department.
3. Any proposed deduction is subject to management review and approval.
4. Performance evaluation results, including output from the internal evaluation system and any automated agent, may be used as input to the review in clause 3.
